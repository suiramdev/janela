# NNNN. Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is true that forces a decision? Constraints, requirements, and the relevant
facts — with citations where the facts are checkable. Someone reading this in a
year should understand the situation without needing the surrounding conversation.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the active voice.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it costs. Be honest about the
downsides; an ADR with no downsides is marketing, not a record.

## Alternatives considered

For each: what it was, and the specific reason it lost. "It was worse" is not a
reason. Include the option we would pick if this decision were reversed.

## Revisit when

The concrete signal that should make someone reopen this. A version, a benchmark
result, a feature request, a maintenance event.
